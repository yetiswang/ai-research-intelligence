AI for Research Intelligence

Building mental models for research support
Yuyang Wang Research Infrastructure Manager · ICMS, TU/e
1 · Why

Everyone I meet carries a different mental model.

Every day I meet people in many roles. Next to technical ideas, we share research intelligence: who works where, with whom, on what. It is about geography and networks, and each person sorts it their own way.

Research support is managing these mental models, and searching for clarity in a complex environment that changes every week.

2 · How · the journey

From a list by hand to 118 initiatives on a map.

Here the AI-assisted work begins
1

A database by hand

Lab names, people, places and funding, collected from talks, papers and conversations.

2

AI agents search the web

Systematic searches country by country, programme by programme, with a source for every find.

3

I check every entry

Each claim traced to its source before it goes in. People make the final call.

4

One record per lab

The six questions become fields: name, people, domain, tools, place, funding.

5

Map, list and timeline

One data file feeds a map, a list and a timeline, in four languages.

6

Weekly watch

New developments each week, and submissions from the community itself.

A research intelligence engine, always ready.

Clarity for everyone

4 · Reflect

Four lessons from building the map.

1

AI scouts, people verify

Agents find far more than one person can. Every entry still passes a human check.

2

Every claim keeps its source

A link on each fact makes the map citable and easy to correct.

3

Choose the tool by the data

Public data in any tool. Internal data in TU/e on-premise models.

4

Start small, add every week

Begin with a list by hand, and improve it a little every week.

Next

Open the map further to the community, and bring the same approach to the ICMS research facilities.
